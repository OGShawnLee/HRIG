# Hostile / Closed Environments

**What it is:** Self-contained spacecraft or habitat ecosystems where air quality, pressure, temperature, lighting, noise, water, and microbes are tightly coupled and recycled.

**Why it matters:**
- Confined, recycled systems can amplify microbial transmission and alter crew immune responses.
- Suboptimal temperature, CO2 levels, noise, or lighting degrade sleep and physiological comfort.
- Requires continuous monitoring of both habitat conditions and crew health indicators.
