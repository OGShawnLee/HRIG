# Gravity Fields

**What it is:** Exposure to altered gravity environments—microgravity in transit/orbit, partial gravity on the Moon or Mars, and hypergravity/1g transitions on launch and return.

**Why it matters:**
- Causes bone density loss and muscle atrophy from reduced mechanical loading.
- Shifts bodily fluids upward and deconditions the cardiovascular system.
- Affects the inner ear (vestibular system), vision, balance, and organ function.
