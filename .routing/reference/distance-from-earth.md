# Distance from Earth

**What it is:** Extreme physical separation from Earth that introduces communication latency, prevents rapid resupply, and eliminates emergency return options.

**Why it matters:**
- Flight surgeons on the ground cannot provide real-time assistance on deep-space missions.
- Medications, food, and medical supplies are strictly limited to what is carried onboard.
- Astronauts must autonomously monitor indicators, assess symptoms, and act early to prevent critical conditions.
