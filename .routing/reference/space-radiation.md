# Space Radiation

**What it is:** Invisible ionizing radiation (including galactic cosmic rays, solar particle events, and neutron radiation) encountered beyond Earth's protective atmosphere and magnetic field.

**Why it matters:**
- Causes cellular and DNA damage, increasing long-term cancer risk and acute biological harm.
- Neutron radiation alone contributes roughly 30% of total radiation exposure on the ISS.
- Contributes to cardiovascular, central nervous system, and immune system changes.
